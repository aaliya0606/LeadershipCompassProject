Understand how others experience your leadership. Invite colleagues and teammates to provide anonymous feedback and use their perspectives to identify strengths and opportunities for growth.

Why 360° feedback?

Your own view of your leadership is only one perspective. Feedback from the people around you can help reveal patterns, strengths and development areas that may otherwise be difficult to see.
